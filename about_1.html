About Marikopo

Our History


Founded by The Salvation Army Zimbabwe Territory to serve rural learners in Marikopo Village, Dema, Seke. From a few blocks to a registered ZIMSEC centre serving Manyame & Seke.


Mission: Affordable, Christ-centered, quality secondary education producing disciplined, innovative, service-oriented citizens.


Vision: Leading rural Salvation Army school of excellence by 2030.

Core Values


• Faith: Christ at centre
• Discipline: Respect, timekeeping
• Excellence: Hard work
• Service: Heart to God, Hand to Man
• Ubuntu: Community & humility
