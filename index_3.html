Welcome from the Head

"Welcome to Marikopo Secondary, a mission school where Truth, Service & Discipline is lived daily. We blend rigorous ZIMSEC academics, Christian formation, and practical skills for learners from Seke, Dema and beyond."


On behalf of The Salvation Army Zimbabwe Territory and the Board of Governors, I warmly welcome you to our school. As a Salvation Army educational institution, we are committed to holistic development — mind, body, and spirit.


At Marikopo, every child is known, valued, and challenged to achieve excellence. Our dedicated staff work tirelessly to ensure high ZIMSEC pass rates while instilling the values of integrity, discipline, and service to humanity — the true Salvation Army ethos of "Heart to God, Hand to Man."


Whether you are a prospective parent, learner, or partner, we invite you to visit us in Marikopo Village and experience our disciplined, caring, and God-fearing community.


God bless you.
— The Headmaster
